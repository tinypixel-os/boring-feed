STORAGE JUSTIFICATION
The extension uses chrome.storage.sync to save the user's settings: the personal facts they type on the Options page, the on/off switch, the shuffle setting and which sites are turned off. The content script reads these settings to build the replacement feed. The data stays in Chrome's own storage and is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

HOST PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION
The extension needs access to linkedin.com, x.com, twitter.com, reddit.com, instagram.com, 9gag.com and youtube.com because its only purpose is to replace the home feed on these sites (and, on YouTube, to hide Shorts). On these sites, a content script hides the site's feed element and puts a generated feed of the user's own facts in its place, styled to match the site. It doesn't read, collect or send page content, cookies or personal data, and it makes no network requests. Access is limited to these seven domains and is not requested for any other site.

SINGLE PURPOSE
Boring Feed replaces the home feed on supported social media sites (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, 9GAG and YouTube) with a feed of facts the user has written about themselves, to make endless scrolling less rewarding. All of its features, including the settings page, the toolbar on/off switch and the per-site switches, only control that feed replacement (on YouTube, together with hiding Shorts).
